Taskflow
==============================
TaskFlow is a project and task management platform designed for small teams and freelancers. It helps users organize projects, manage tasks, track deadlines, and monitor project progress in one centralized workspace.
===================================
Target users of this product are:
1- Progect owners: Users who create projects, manage team members, assign tasks, and monitor project progress.

2-Team Members: Users who view their assigned tasks, update task statuses, and track their responsibilities.
======================================
Problem Statement

Small teams and freelancers often use multiple tools to manage projects, tasks, deadlines, and team activities. This makes it difficult to keep information organized, track responsibilities, and monitor project progress.
=========================================
Proposed Solution

TaskFlow provides a centralized workspace where users can manage projects, assign tasks, set priorities and deadlines, and track progress through dashboards and Kanban boards.
==============================================
Key Differentiators

Unlike a simple To-do List, TaskFlow supports project-based task management, team collaboration, task assignment, deadline tracking, and project analytics.
